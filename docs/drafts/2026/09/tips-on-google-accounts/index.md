**Things on this page are fragmentary and immature notes/thoughts of the author. Please read with your own judgement!**

1. Google account supports family group 
    which allow sharing features in one Google account 
    with other accounts in the same family group.
    For example,
    Google One storage and Google AI Pro subscription can be shared to accounts in the family group.

2. Google Photo can be shared any other Google account (not necessarily in the same family group) that you set up.
