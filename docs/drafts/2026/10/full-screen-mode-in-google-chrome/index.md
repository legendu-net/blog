There are several ways to enter the full screen mode of Google Chrome in macOS.

- Use the shortcut `fn` + `ctrl` + `command` + `f`.
- Click on the green circle on the top-left corner of Google Chrome.
- Use the menu: View -> enter/exit full screen.
