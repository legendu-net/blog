**Things on this page are fragmentary and immature notes/thoughts of the author. Please read with your own judgement!**

If you enjoy [Rust Trends](https://rust-trends.com/) (a biweekly, developer-first curated newsletter with low hype and high technical signal) and want the closest equivalents for AI engineering and research, there is no literal `ai-trends.com` sister publication from the same author. As of November 2026, `ai-trends.com` is not an active newsletter and `aitrends.com` is suspended.

## Verdict: Closest Equivalents to Rust Trends

For **low-hype, engineering-first, high-signal** AI curation:

1. **[Latent Space](https://latent.space/)** — Closest in **AI-engineering & ecosystem focus** (agents, inference/infra, dev tools, and model releases). Subscribe to weekly deep dives/podcasts and opt out of daily AINews if you want low inbox volume.
2. **[Ahead of AI](https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/)** — Closest in **calm cadence (biweekly–monthly) & technical rigor** (LLM architectures, training/post-training, and paper syntheses with zero hype).
3. **[Simon Willison's Newsletter](https://simonwillison.substack.com/)** — Closest in **pragmatic developer tooling** (hands-on LLM/agent workflows, CLI tools, and engineering notes from `simonwillison.net`).

## Recommended Minimal Subscription Stack

For engineers working across Rust, Python, and AI agents:

- **Weekly/Biweekly Technical Anchor**: **Ahead of AI** (for deep LLM/ML engineering) *or* **Latent Space** (weekly deep dives only, opting out of daily emails).
- **Practical Tooling Feed**: **Simon Willison's Newsletter** (or RSS from `simonwillison.net`) for hands-on agent/LLM tooling and CLI patterns.
- **Optional Weekday Pulse**: [AINews](https://news.smol.ai/) or [TLDR AI](https://tldr.tech/ai) only if you want a fast weekday scan of new repos and releases.
