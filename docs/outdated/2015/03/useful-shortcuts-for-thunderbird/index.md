## Message

1. F8: Message Preview Pane
1. CTRL + N: New email
1. CTRL + O: Open email
1. CTRL + SHIFT + O: Open in conversation
1. CTRL + U: Email source
1. CTRL + R: Reply to an email
1. CTRL + SHIFT + R: Reply to all
1. CTRL + L: Forward email
1. CTRL + E: Edit email as new
1. CTRL + ENTER: Send current email
1. CTRL + SHIFT + ENTER: Send email later (you need the SendLater add-on)

## Misc

1. CTRL + W: Close window
1. CTRL + SHIFT + M: Move again
1. CTRL + SHIFT + B: Open address book

## Menu

1. ALT + F: pop up the "File" menu
1. ALT + E: pop up the "Edit" menu
1. ALT + V: pop up the "View" menu
1. ALT + S: pop up the "History" menu
1. ALT + B: pop up the "Bookmark" menu
1. ALT + T: Pop up the "Tool" menu.
1. ALT + H: pop up the "Help" menu
